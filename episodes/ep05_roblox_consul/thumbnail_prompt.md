---

A dramatic, photorealistic dark editorial image. On a concrete warehouse floor under a
single hard overhead work light: on the right, one very small cardboard parcel, no bigger
than a shoebox, sitting alone. On the left, towering over it and running out of the top of
the frame, an enormous unstable stack of white paper sheets, easily twenty times the
parcel's height, slightly leaning, a few loose sheets caught mid-fall. Both objects sit on
the same floor with hard contact shadows so the size difference is unmistakable. Deep
near-black background (#151921) falling off into darkness, cold blue-grey ambience, one
warm amber pool of light from above. Muted, desaturated, filmic, high contrast, heavy
negative space in the upper right for text. No people, no logos, no screens, no text
anywhere in the image itself.

Overlay text, in a heavy bold condensed sans, off-white (#F2F0EA), upper right, two lines,
the second line noticeably smaller:

**THEY MADE IT FASTER**
**73 HOURS OFFLINE**

---

Binding rules:

- Palette: near-black ground `#151921`, off-white type `#F2F0EA`, amber accent `#F5A623`,
  red only if something must read as failure `#FF4757`. No other hues.
- 16:9, 1280x720 minimum.
- **No logos and no brand marks** — not Roblox's, not HashiCorp's, not anyone's.
- **No fabricated artefacts**: no invented screenshots, dashboards, terminals, error
  messages or documents. The paper stack is blank sheets, not printed pages.
- The parcel must read as *small* at a glance. If the two objects look comparable in size,
  the image has failed and the stack needs to be taller.
- Check it at **168x94** — if "73 HOURS OFFLINE" is not readable at that size, the type is
  too small or too thin.
